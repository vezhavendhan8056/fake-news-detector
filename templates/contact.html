FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the fake news detector?
Our hybrid framework yields approximately 66.67% accuracy across our test suite. It achieves this by combining vocabulary features parsed through a Logistic Regression style model (35% weight) with real-time web source agreement (65% weight) evaluated through live searches.
What languages does it support?
The current implementation is designed for English-language claims, as the stopword filtration algorithms and underlying vectorization models were trained on English political publications.
How long should the article or claim be?
For search keyword extraction to formulate precise queries, claims of 10 to 50 words perform best. Full-length articles can also be analysed since the system automatically selects key nouns and subject indicators.
Is my data stored or shared?
All verification history is stored locally in standard JSON files on the server for the history feature. No data is shared with third parties. This is a local application for educational purposes.
Can I use this for my own project?
Yes! This platform is open-source. You are welcome to adapt, modify, or extend the source code for your academic portfolios.
