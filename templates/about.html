About the Project

Making Information
Easier to Verify.

TruthLens is an AI-powered fake news detection system built as a B.Sc. Information Technology and Cybersecurity using machine learning and natural language processing.

Mission

Why This Matters

The rapid growth of digital media has made the spread of misinformation a growing concern. Fake news, manipulated narratives, and misleading information can shape public opinion, influence elections, and pose serious risks to society. TruthLens was developed to offer an accessible and practical solution for assessing the credibility of news content.

TruthLens employs machine learning techniques trained on thousands of verified authentic and fabricated news articles to detect linguistic patterns commonly associated with misleading information. By analyzing these patterns, the system assists users in assessing news credibility and making informed decisions before reading or sharing content.

Technology

How Machine Learning Detects Fake News

🔤

Text Preprocessing

Raw article text is cleaned by removing URLs, HTML tags, special characters, and English stop words. Words are then stemmed to their root forms to reduce dimensionality and improve model generalisation.

📊

TF-IDF Vectorisation

Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency transforms text into numerical feature vectors. With 50,000 features using both unigrams and bigrams, the model captures word importance and contextual phrases that distinguish real from fake content.

🤖

Logistic Regression Classifier

A Logistic Regression classifier is trained on an 80/20 split of the dataset. This model outputs probability scores for both classes (Real/Fake), allowing TruthLens to provide not just a verdict but a quantified confidence level for every prediction.

💾

Model Persistence

The trained model and TF-IDF vectorizer are serialised using Joblib, allowing them to be loaded once at server startup and reused for every prediction without retraining. The Flask REST API handles all prediction requests.

Dataset

Training Data

TruthLens is trained on the Kaggle Fake and Real News Dataset, which contains over 40,000 political news articles classified as either genuine or fabricated. The dataset consists of approximately 21,000 fake news articles and 21,000 real news articles published between 2016 and 2017.

Limitations

Important Considerations

  • ⚠ English only. TruthLens was trained exclusively on English text. Other languages will produce unreliable results.
  • ⚠ Not a replacement for journalism. Machine learning supplements human judgment. Always verify important claims with multiple trusted sources.
  • ⚠ Training bias. The model was trained on political news from a specific time period. Its performance on other topics or newer content may vary.
  • ⚠ Short text limitations. Predictions on very short texts (<50 words) may be significantly less accurate than those on full articles.
